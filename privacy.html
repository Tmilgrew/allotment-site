PayPots

Privacy Policy

Effective September 24, 2026

PayPots is an iPhone app for tracking savings allocations. This policy explains what happens to the information you enter. The short version: it stays on your phone.

What PayPots collects

Nothing. PayPots does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information or usage data. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash reporting. It never connects to the internet.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter in PayPots, including account names, allocation names, balances, contribution amounts, paycheck dates, and the ledger of every balance change, is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Backups

Because the data lives in the app's storage, it is included in your device backups. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or to a computer, your PayPots data is part of that backup and is handled under Apple's backup terms. PayPots does not use iCloud sync of its own.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app from your iPhone deletes all PayPots data with it. There is no copy anywhere else to remove. Restoring from a device backup taken before the deletion would bring the data back.

Children

PayPots does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If PayPots ever changes how it handles your data, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.
