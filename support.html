Common questions

How do balances grow?

Each allocation has a per-paycheck contribution. Every time a payday passes, Allotment adds that contribution to the allocation's balance and records the change in its ledger. Open Settings to set your payday and how often you are paid.

What if I open the app after several paydays?

Allotment catches up. Every payday that passed since you last opened the app is applied, one ledger entry per payday, so balances land where they would have anyway.

Can I track a partner's paycheck?

Yes. Settings has a second paycheck schedule. Accounts marked as shared grow on both paychecks; other accounts grow only on yours.

Is my data backed up?

Allotment keeps everything on your iPhone and never sends it anywhere. Your data is included in your normal iCloud or computer backup. Deleting the app deletes the data. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

Restore the new phone from a backup of the old one. Allotment's data comes across with the rest of the app data.
